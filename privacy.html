HEOS — Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

HEOS is a private application used only by its owner's household. It is not distributed to the public. This policy explains what HEOS accesses when a household member signs in with Google, and how that data is handled.

What HEOS accesses

  • YouTube: the signed-in account's liked videos and playlists, used to understand music taste. HEOS may create playlists in the account when the owner asks it to.
  • Gmail: message metadata and short previews, used to sort and prioritize the inbox. HEOS may create draft replies. HEOS may apply labels or archive messages only after the owner reviews and approves each change. HEOS never sends email without the owner's explicit review and approval, and it never deletes email.
  • Google Calendar: read-only access to events, used for daily briefings. HEOS does not create, change, or delete events.

How data is handled

  • Data is processed and stored only on devices and servers owned and controlled by the owner.
  • Data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Data is not sent to third-party AI services and is not used to train generalized AI models.
  • Access credentials are stored with owner-only permissions, and every action HEOS takes is recorded in an audit log.

Google API Services

HEOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, the owner deletes all data HEOS holds for an account.
